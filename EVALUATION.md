## Diagnosis

The primary diagnosis matched the true incident type in 98.2% of questions, and 91.2% of all true incident types were named somewhere in the diagnosis.

## Held out phrasing

A second bank of 30 questions, written after the thesaurus was last tuned and never used for tuning, measures how well understanding generalises. Hybrid precision at 10 was 0.867, the primary diagnosis was correct in 86.7% of questions and the top fix was genuinely strong in 86.7%.
